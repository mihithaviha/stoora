Privacy policy

This is a plain-language draft of what Stoora stores. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before you launch to the public.

What we store

Sellers: name, email, shop details, products, orders and your buyers' names, phones and delivery addresses. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

What we do with it

We use it to run your shop: showing products, taking orders, sending order and account emails, and showing you your sales. We do not sell your data.

Who else sees it

Razorpay processes payments. The email provider you configure sends account and order emails. If you turn on the assistant, the questions you ask are sent to the AI provider.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your products and shop details in the dashboard. To delete your account and data, contact us from the address in Settings.
